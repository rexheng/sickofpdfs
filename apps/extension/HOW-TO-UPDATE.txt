HOW TO UPDATE sickofpdfs WITHOUT LOSING YOUR NOTES
===================================================

Your readings, marks, comments, tags and settings live inside Chrome,
under this extension's name. Updating by replacing the files and
pressing Reload keeps all of them.

Do this each time you get a new test build (Windows):

1. Keep ONE folder for sickofpdfs, for example C:\sickofpdfs.
   Always load the extension from that same folder. Never make a
   second copy somewhere else.

2. Close any sickofpdfs tabs that are still open (the library and any
   PDF you are reading). If you were typing a note, it is kept safe
   and comes back, but closing the tabs first is calmer.

3. Unzip the new test build into that same folder, C:\sickofpdfs.
   When Windows asks, choose "Replace the files in the destination".

4. Open Chrome and go to:   chrome://extensions
   Find sickofpdfs and press the round reload arrow on its card.

5. Open your library (click the sickofpdfs icon). You should see:
   "Updated to test build N. Your library is safe."

NEVER press "Remove" on the sickofpdfs card.
Remove deletes your whole library and all your notes. They cannot be
brought back from Chrome. The same happens if you remove the extension
and add it again.

For extra safety, turn on a backup before you update:
   Library -> Settings -> Backup.
Pick a folder, and your library is copied there. If anything ever goes
wrong, Settings -> Restore brings it back.

Why one folder: Chrome remembers an unpacked extension by its folder.
If you load it from a second folder, Chrome may ask you to remove the
first one, and removing it deletes the library.
