HOW TO UPDATE sickofpdfs WITHOUT LOSING YOUR NOTES
===================================================

Your readings, marks, comments, tags and settings live inside Chrome,
under this extension's name. Updating by replacing the files and
pressing Reload keeps all of them.

The one thing that can lose them is the Remove button. Never press it.
(More about it at the bottom.)

Do this each time you get a new test build.


STEP 1. Use ONE folder.
   Keep one folder for sickofpdfs, for example C:\sickofpdfs.
   Chrome always loads the extension from that same folder.
   Never make a second copy somewhere else.

STEP 2. Close the sickofpdfs tabs.
   Close the library and any PDF you are reading.
   If you were typing a note, it is kept safe and comes back.
   Closing the tabs first is calmer.

STEP 3. Put the new files into that same folder.

   On Windows:
     a. Find the zip you were sent. Right-click it.
     b. Click "Extract All...".
     c. Windows suggests a new folder named after the zip.
        Do NOT use it. Press "Browse...".
     d. Pick your sickofpdfs folder (C:\sickofpdfs).
        Press "Select Folder".
     e. The box must now show C:\sickofpdfs and nothing after it.
     f. Press "Extract".
     g. When Windows says the files already exist, click
        "Replace the files in the destination".

   On a Mac:
     a. Double-click the zip. It opens into a new folder.
     b. Open that new folder. Select everything inside it (Cmd+A).
     c. Drag it all into your sickofpdfs folder.
     d. When Finder asks, press "Replace".

   Check it. Open your sickofpdfs folder.
   Afterwards the folder must hold "manifest.json" directly,
   not another folder.
   If you only see a folder (for example "sickofpdfs-test-build-7"),
   the files went one level too deep. Open that folder, select
   everything inside it, and move it up into your sickofpdfs folder,
   replacing the files.

STEP 4. Press Reload.
   Open Chrome. Type  chrome://extensions  in the address bar and
   press Enter.
   Find the sickofpdfs card.
   Press Reload. It is the small round arrow at the bottom right of the
   card, next to the on/off switch.
   Reload is the only button you need. Do not press anything else.
   The card now says the new build after "sickofpdfs",
   for example "Test build 7".

STEP 5. Open your library.
   Click the sickofpdfs icon in Chrome's toolbar.
   In the menu that opens, click "Open library".
   On the Home page you should see:
      "Updated to test build N. Your library is safe."
   It shows only once for each update.
   If you do not see it, the new files did not load. Check Step 3 and
   press Reload again. Do not press Remove.


NEVER PRESS "REMOVE".
   Remove is the button with the word "Remove" on the sickofpdfs card,
   beside "Details".
   It deletes your whole library and all your notes. They cannot be
   brought back from Chrome.
   The same happens if you remove the extension and add it again.


FOR EXTRA SAFETY
   Turn on a backup before you update:
      Library -> Settings -> Backup.
   Pick a folder, and your library is copied there. If anything ever
   goes wrong, Settings -> Restore brings it back.


WHY ONE FOLDER
   So that there is only ever one sickofpdfs, and you always know which
   one it is. We have not tested what Chrome does when the same
   extension is loaded from two different folders. So do not do it.
